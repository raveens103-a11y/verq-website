Our Story

Built by an HR veteran
who got tired of
broken consultancies.

After 8+ years in recruitment across tech, FMCG, and mobility — Naveen Singh built the platform he always wished existed.

"Every founder I met after their funding round had the same problem — they needed great people, fast, without paying a consultancy's ransom."

— Naveen Singh, Founder of verQ

verQ started from a simple frustration. After spending 8+ years placing talent across tech startups, FMCG brands, and mobility companies — I kept seeing the same pattern repeat itself.

Funded startups with real money, real ambition, and zero HR support. Traditional consultancies charging 8–10% of CTC, disappearing after sending a pile of CVs, and never being held accountable for the hire working out.

HR freelancers with real skills but no platform to find consistent, well-paying work. No credibility. No visibility. No fair deal.

verQ is the fix. A marketplace that treats both sides as professionals, holds itself accountable with guarantees, and charges a flat fee that doesn't penalise you for hiring well.

The Founder
Naveen Singh
Founder & CEO, verQ
With 8+ years in recruitment and HR across tech startups, FMCG, retail, and the mobility sector — Naveen has personally placed hundreds of professionals across functions and levels. He has worked with early-stage startups making their first 10 hires and with scaled companies managing 500+ person teams.

Having seen the HR freelance market from both sides — as a practitioner and as someone who has hired extensively — Naveen built verQ to solve a problem he lived firsthand: great HR talent exists, great companies exist, but there's no trusted platform to connect them quickly, fairly, and without the traditional consultancy markup.
